Meet Our Doctor

Dr. Meera Anand, DDS

Lead Dentist & Founder, Cosmetic & Implant Specialist

“I believe dentistry should feel like being cared for, not processed. Every plan we write starts with listening.”

Dr. Anand completed her DDS at the University of Michigan School of Dentistry, followed by an advanced fellowship in prosthodontics. She founded Aurelia Dental House in 2011 to combine rigorous clinical standards with an unhurried, patient-first experience, and has since led the clinic through more than 12,000 patient treatments.

  • DDS — University of Michigan School of Dentistry
  • Fellowship in Prosthodontics — advanced restorative & implant care
